# Stakeholder & User Feedback Validation Summary

This document captures early prototype validation feedback from key security and leadership stakeholders.

---

### Persona 1: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
* **Pre-Prototype Pain Point:** *"I receive 50-page PDF vulnerability exports every Monday from three teams. I cannot tell our CEO if our risk is getting better or worse."*
* **Validation Feedback on Prototype:**
  * ✅ *"The single Business Risk Index (0-100) and the 'Risk Reduction Attributable to Controls' KPI give me board-ready numbers instantly."*
  * ✅ *"Being able to see risk by Asset Tier (Tier 1 Payment DB vs Tier 3 QA) ensures we prioritize budget where business impact is highest."*
* **Score Given:** 9.5 / 10

---

### Persona 2: Lead Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst
* **Pre-Prototype Pain Point:** *"We waste hours chasing alerts on test servers while critical production alerts get lost in tool noise."*
* **Validation Feedback on Prototype:**
  * ✅ *"The Freshness Indicators immediately alert us when a tool's agent goes stale (>24h), avoiding visibility gaps."*
  * ✅ *"Filtering alerts by Control and Asset Tier lets us drill down directly to the root cause without switching between 5 tool consoles."*
* **Score Given:** 9.0 / 10

---

### Persona 3: Cloud Infrastructure / DevOps Engineer
* **Pre-Prototype Pain Point:** *"Security team tells us to enable controls, but if it breaks production traffic, we cannot revert quickly."*
* **Validation Feedback on Prototype:**
  * ✅ *"The Control Sandbox and 1-Click Rollback button provide a safety net for testing high-impact changes with complete audit logging."*
* **Score Given:** 9.2 / 10
